About Me LinkedIn

Libo Huang is a full-time, tenure-track Assistant Professor (Assistant Researcher) at the Institute of Computing Technology (ICT), Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS). He can solely advise Ph.D. and master's students in Computer Science. Prior to joining the faculty, he worked as a postdoctoral researcher (2021.10 - 2024.8) and an intern researcher (2021.7-2021.10) at ICT, CAS, where he collaborated with Prof. Yongjun Xu and Prof. Zhulin An on deep learning research.

He received his Ph.D. degree in Machine Learning under the joint supervision of Prof. Wing-Kuen Ling at Guangdong University of Technology (GDUT) (2018-2021) and Prof. Lu Gan at Brunel University of London (2019-2020). Before that, he earned his M.Eng. degree in Computer Science from GDUT (2016-2018), supervised by Prof. Zhifeng Hao and Prof. Ruichu Cai, and a B.S. degree in Mathematics from Jiangxi Normal University (2012-2016).

Research Interests Team Website

His core research focuses on Continual Learning in Open Environments, bridging the gap between fundamental mechanisms and real-world deployment to build reliable, efficient, and self-evolving AI systems. Specifically, his interests span:
  • Machine Cognition & Efficient Foundation Models: Investigating continual discriminative and generative learning mechanisms, and leveraging model compression techniques (e.g., knowledge distillation and quantization) for efficient representation in open scenarios.
  • Scene Perception & Multimodal Learning: Tackling 3D few-shot continual learning within noisy and data-scarce physical spaces (e.g., 3D Point Cloud analysis), and exploring the long-term collaborative evolution of multimodal knowledge across vision and language.
  • Theoretical Foundations & Causal Interpretability: Tracing the underlying mechanisms of catastrophic forgetting and exploring the causal origins of knowledge accumulation (Causal Learning) to empower AI systems with interpretable memory capabilities.
