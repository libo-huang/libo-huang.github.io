About Me LinkedIn

I am a full-time, tenure-track Assistant Professor (Assistant Researcher) at the Institute of Computing Technology (ICT), Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS). I can solely advise Ph.D. and master's students in Computer Science. Prior to joining the faculty, I worked as a postdoctoral researcher (2021.10-2024.8) and an intern researcher (2021.7-2021.10) at ICT, CAS, where I collaborated with Prof. Yongjun Xu and Prof. Zhulin An on deep learning research.

I received my Ph.D. degree in Machine Learning under the joint supervision of Prof. Wing-Kuen Ling at Guangdong University of Technology (GDUT) (2018.09-2021.07) and Prof. Lu Gan at Brunel University of London (2019.10-2020.10). Before that, I earned my M.Eng. degree in Computer Science from GDUT (2016.09-2018.07), supervised by Prof. Zhifeng Hao and Prof. Ruichu Cai, and a B.S. degree in Mathematics from Jiangxi Normal University (2012.09-2016.07).

Research Interests Team Website

My research focuses on Continual Learning in Open-World Environments, bridging the gap between causal theoretical foundations and real-world deployment to build self-evolving Continual AI systems anchored in scene perception, machine cognition, and causal learning. Specifically:

  • Scene-Perception Loop: Tackling 3D few-shot continual learning within noisy and data-scarce physical scenes, and exploring the long-term collaborative evolution of multimodal knowledge across vision and language.
  • Machine-Cognition Loop: Investigating continual discriminative and generative machine learning mechanisms, and leveraging model compression techniques for efficient representation in open scenarios.
  • Causal-Learning Loop: Tracing the underlying mechanisms of catastrophic forgetting and exploring the causal origins of knowledge accumulation to empower AI systems with interpretable memory capabilities.
